## Display and matching rules

Cards and intelligence distinguish current list benchmarks, historical lists, original currency/market and base-reference limitations. Exact dates, year/month or publication-year precision are displayed as supported. Historical values do not become current MSRP, discount/appreciation calculations or current U.S. budget matches.

The summary groups by currency, market, scope and historical period before choosing a range. It never compares a lower euro or Swiss-franc number with a U.S. dollar number. `referenceAliases` records explicit photographed full-SKU equivalence for Freak X, Freak ONE and Blast. Each observation remains unique; existing pages stay in the registry while search recommends the full reference once. Duplicate copies of the same brand/reference across collections are also consolidated in recommendations, using existing route helpers.
