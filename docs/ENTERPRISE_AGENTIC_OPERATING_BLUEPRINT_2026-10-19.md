# Quest Luxo Enterprise Agentic Operating Blueprint
Date: 2026-10-19 (America/New_York). Status: PROPOSED planning document; not an assertion that any new agents or live automations have been deployed.

## Operating objective
Operate Quest Luxo Luxury Advisory, Quest Luxo Realty/Property Acquisitions, and Quest Luxo Capital Advisory from a single evidence-backed enterprise command system. Maximize qualified leads, closings, contribution margin and collected cash while minimizing founder routine work. Multimillion-dollar revenue/cash-flow aspirations are targets, not forecasts.

## Workflow boundaries
1. Intake -> trusted source receipt -> source identity -> canonical contact/opportunity -> provenance and consent review -> qualification -> division queue -> prioritized next-best-action.
2. Each division owns underwriting/sourcing/matching, evidence collection, proposed commercial terms, transaction milestones and reconciliation of collected revenue.
3. Shared marketing owns brand, approved content and measured acquisition across divisions. Shared finance owns ledger verification, invoice/fee reconciliation and cash reporting.
4. Founder Command owns approvals, exception handling, delegation and operating dashboards. Every action has owner, event ID, evidence links, state, timestamp and audit log.
5. Fail closed: missing/contradictory evidence becomes UNKNOWN/CONFLICT; dedupe and idempotent retries prevent duplicate leads, messages and transactions.

## Control tiers
- **Tier A: Internal automation allowed after QA:** read/normalize evidence, deduplicate, label/rank, draft, calculate, flag anomalies, run tests, create internal reminders and dashboards.
- **Tier B: Scoped, revocable founder opt-in:** send pre-approved low-risk communications or publish approved content via verified platform integration with recipient/channel limits, logging, rate limits and human override.
- **Tier C: Human/counsel approval required:** credit pulls and lender submissions, bank and financial access, loan offers, real-estate offers/contracts, guarantees, deposits, funds movement, vendor obligations, regulated representations, broad external campaigns and credential changes.
- No agents may promise approvals, financing, property returns, guaranteed investment results, product authenticity, watch availability or bank balances without evidence.
- State and local real estate, lending, privacy, advertising, and referral-fee rules require relevant professional review before production operation. No assumption of national licensing coverage.

## Minimum release gates for each sprint
- Clean isolated branch, unit/integration/security tests, full regression and production build, protected PR review; no destructive checkout changes or duplicate project.
- Proven source identity, private credentials, consent/access scope, fixed audit event IDs, idempotency, UNKNOWN and CONFLICT behavior, and no invented economic numbers.
- Synthetic and founder-authorized controlled end-to-end validation, observable failures, restoration/rollback and live release confirmation separate from code merge.
- Business metrics: captured vs received lead reconciliation, qualified conversion, time to first response, time to close/fund, collected gross profit and cash, receivables days, incidents, founder approval volume/time, cost per qualified lead and cost per closed deal.
- No broad external publishing, mass outreach, credit access, contracts or payment automation until separately approved and compliance reviewed.
