# Functional workforce operating model

The workforce uses six accountable teams. The historical 57-role catalog remains a capability inventory and integrity reference; it is not a target for 57 independent processes, services or paid model sessions. The existing Command Orchestrator, canonical division stores, intake forms and integration boundaries remain in place.

| Accountable team | Historical capabilities | Responsibility |
|---|---:|---|
| Executive Command & Orchestration | 5 | Prioritization, reporting and approval escalation |
| Technology & Luxury Advisory | 12 | Luxury mandate qualification and product engineering |
| Marketing & Growth | 9 | Growth requirements and handoffs to publishing owners |
| Capital Advisory & Funding | 8 | Evidence and package readiness |
| Property Acquisitions | 7 | Property screening and acquisition preparation |
| Enterprise Operations | 16 | CRM identity, recovery, security and shared operations |

Run `npm run agent:factory -- teams` to validate the one-team-per-capability inventory. `roster` remains available for historical package compatibility. Runtime delivery stages are unknown in this inventory until supported by evidence: a historical catalog's status cannot prove deployment. Delivery evidence must distinguish SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, DEPLOYED and OPERATIONALLY_VERIFIED. Team count does not count independent workers.

The existing pilot command report adds accountable team and execution workstream to its bounded recommendations. Its health response exposes operating model version and team count separately from observed operational roles. Existing public monitoring and recovery holds remain authoritative.

`src/lib/command/workforceQueue.ts` projects bounded metadata into the existing Command queue across Capture → Verify → Qualify → Route → Follow Up → Advance → Measure → Report. It validates explicit source, revision, evidence and freshness; rejects unsupported or personal-data fields; deduplicates repeat delivery; and holds conflicting or stale observations. It does not ingest source records, persist an outbox, advance a pipeline or prove completeness. Lead conversion, completed business tasks and founder time saved stay null until measured.

Routine internal follow-up preparation belongs to its division team. Transaction review remains an approval escalation. SEO implementation and website content deployment route to workstream A; LinkedIn and social publishing route to B. Current workstream C owns orchestration, reliability, shared interfaces and internal metadata preparation. A handoff never grants this workstream authority over an unseen chat, deployment or publication.

The CRM metadata planner is described in `CRM_RECONCILIATION_INTERFACE.md`. Its luxury division label `LUXURY_ADVISORY` corresponds to the existing Command division `WATCH_BROKERAGE`; callers must explicitly map those labels at the reviewed source boundary. No identifier match, native analytics field or email tracking consent substitutes for source identity or processing authorization. The planner cannot write CRM records or dispatch communications. Existing canonical sources and form destinations remain authoritative.

Validate with `node --test tests/workforceOperatingModel.test.mjs tests/crmReconciliationPlan.test.mjs tests/pilotCommandOrchestrator.test.mjs`. Division decision packages remain synthetic previews as documented in `AGENT_FACTORY_DIVISION_PREVIEWS.md`. Business execution requires current independently recovered backups, recurring retention and reviewed source mappings and scope. Paid model execution remains disabled at a zero-dollar budget.
